# SEO Recommendations — Keyword Gap Analysis (2026-07-24)

Based on `results/serp-2026-07-24.jsonl` (SerpApi Google search monitor). All five non-branded tracked keywords currently show exadel.com not ranking in the top 10.

**Pattern across all five:** Exadel has relevant *expertise* (AI adoption, GenAI, LLM governance, chatbots) but it's scattered across blog/news posts (`exadel.com/news/...`) rather than dedicated, intent-matched service landing pages. Every competitor ranking in the top 3–5 has a purpose-built page whose title near-exactly matches the query. That's the structural gap to close before anything else.

---

## 1. "AI development company" — ScienceSoft #2, Coherent Solutions #1, Appinventiv #4

- No dedicated `exadel.com/ai-development-company` or equivalent page exists — closest is `/services/ai-powered-applications/`, which doesn't target this phrase.
- **Action:** Create/retarget a top-level services page with H1 "AI Development Company" (or "AI Development Services") as the primary heading, not just a mention. Include: service breakdown, industries served, client logos/case studies, team size (2,000+ engineers is a real differentiator vs. boutique competitors — surface it above the fold).
- Competing pages are heavy on proof points (client counts, delivery models, tech stack lists) — mirror that structure.
- Add to primary nav / footer so it's crawlable from the homepage in ≤2 clicks.

## 2. "generative AI consulting services" — N-iX #3, Thackett Group #1, Gartner #2

- Exadel's GenAI content exists but is narrowly scoped to CDD/KYC banking use cases (`/news/...`) — good for a niche, but doesn't answer the broader "consulting services" query.
- **Action:** Build a standalone `/services/generative-ai-consulting/` page generalized beyond finance — cover the consulting *methodology* (assessment → vendor/model selection → pilot → scale), not just one vertical outcome. Link the existing banking/CDD content as a case study/proof point under this page rather than as the primary landing target.
- N-iX's ranking page title is literally "Generative AI consulting for business innovation" — match that directness in title tag and H1.

## 3. "LLM integration consulting" — Accenture #2 (moved from #6 in the prior run — worth re-auditing sooner given the swing)

- Exadel actually has strong substance here (LLM governance gateway, cost optimization guide, Exadel Colleague) but it's fragmented across separate blog posts with no unifying "LLM integration" service page.
- **Action:** Create a consolidated `/services/llm-integration/` (or `/solutions/llm-integration/`) page that rolls up governance, cost optimization, and Colleague as sub-sections/proof points under one URL targeting this exact phrase. This is the strongest content-reuse opportunity of the five — least new content needed, mostly consolidation + a new landing page + internal links from the existing posts.

## 4. "custom AI chatbot development company" — Appinventiv #1 (just took over from EffectiveSoft — active turnover, competitive term)

- Exadel's chatbot content is all "Innovation Lab" / PoC-framed, not positioned as a productized service. No page targets "chatbot development company" as a commercial phrase.
- **Action:** Create `/services/ai-chatbot-development/` with clear commercial framing (not just "we ran a PoC") — service tiers, integration options (site/voice/Slack/Teams), example builds, timeline/cost ballpark. This keyword just changed hands between two mid-size competitors, meaning the SERP is unsettled — a well-optimized page has a realistic shot at entering top 10 faster here than on more entrenched terms.

## 5. "AI software engineering services for enterprises" — Cognizant #3, GlobalLogic #1

- Closest existing match is `/solutions/ai-adoption` and `/services/ai-powered-applications/`, neither titled for this phrase.
- **Action:** Either retarget `/services/ai-powered-applications/` with this phrase as a secondary H2 target, or build a dedicated enterprise-focused page emphasizing "software engineering" (not just "applications") — enterprise buyers searching this phrase want engineering rigor/scale signals (25+ years, 2,000+ engineers, 100+ enterprise clients) foregrounded, similar to how GlobalLogic and Cognizant lead with delivery scale.

---

## Cross-cutting priorities

1. **Consolidate before creating** — several keywords (esp. #3) can be addressed by merging existing blog content into new landing pages rather than writing from scratch.
2. **Title/H1 alignment** — every ranking competitor page's title tag near-matches the query verbatim; audit Exadel's existing pages for this before publishing new ones.
3. **Internal linking** — link these new/retargeted service pages from the homepage nav and from the related `/news/` posts (currently the reverse is missing — posts don't point to a commercial landing page because none exists).
4. **Re-check "LLM integration consulting" and "custom AI chatbot development company" sooner** than the rest — both showed ranking volatility between the last two runs, meaning the SERPs are less settled and a new page has better odds of breaking in.
5. This addresses the Google channel only — none of this content work will move the AI-assistant (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini) organic mention rate, since those draw from training data and broader web presence/citations rather than on-page SEO alone. That gap needs a separate strategy (press coverage, third-party listicles/directories like Clutch, analyst mentions) if it's meant to be tackled too.
